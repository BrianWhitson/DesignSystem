Rodeo Rivals

A Hope Ranch Romance (Hope Ranch Romance, Twin Arrows, Montana)

  • Western Romance
  • Hope Ranch Romance

I came to the Oklahoma County Fair with two goals: find a second horse, and prove myself on the western circuit.

I found the horse the moment I laid eyes on her. A deep sorrel mare with fire in her eyes and more heart than I'd ever seen in an animal. She was everything I'd been looking for.

The problem was Carson Armstrong had seen the same thing.

He's an all-around rodeo champion with a finance degree, a Montana ranch in his blood, and the most infuriating habit of being right about horses.

We sparred over that mare from the first handshake, and somewhere between the dusty practice arenas and a late-night diner booth, the lines between rivalry and something far more dangerous started to blur.

He's good at everything. Reading horses. Reading people. Reading me.

I came here to win. I didn't come here to fall.

But the heart, like a good horse, goes where it decides to go.

Rodeo Rivals A Hope Ranch Romance is the third book in the Hope Ranch series, set in Twin Arrows, Montana.
