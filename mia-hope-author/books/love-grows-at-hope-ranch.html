Love Grows at Hope Ranch

A Small-Town Slow Burn Fresh Start Romance (Hope Ranch Romance, Twin Arrows, Montana)

  • Small Town Romance
  • Hope Ranch Romance

I came to Twin Arrows, Montana to start over. Falling for a stubborn rancher was never part of the plan.

After a painful divorce, a fresh start in a quiet ranching town sounds exactly like what I need. I have a new job running Twin Arrows Veterinary Clinic, a loyal Boykin Spaniel named Cara at my side, and every intention of keeping my life simple.

The only complication is Adam Carter.

Tall, broad-shouldered, and permanently skeptical of my modern veterinary ideas, Adam owns Hope Ranch and the clinic where I work. Every conversation becomes a quiet battle of wills.

His sweet six-year-old daughter Lily steals my heart in minutes. And Adam himself begins to reveal a quieter, kinder side that makes it impossible for me to keep my distance.

Slowly, Twin Arrows starts to feel like home.

Then my past arrives in town, threatening everything I have rebuilt. Including the fragile trust growing between Adam and me.

I don't know what's scarier---that Twin Arrows is starting to feel like home or that Adam is!
