Love Blooms at Hope Ranch

A Small-Town Opposites Attract Clean and Wholesome Romance (Hope Ranch Romance, Twin Arrows, Montana)

  • Clean Small Town Romance
  • Hope Ranch Romance

I came to Twin Arrows, Montana with a truck full of flower plugs, a notebook nearly full of questions, and the stubborn belief that I could build something real from six acres of fallow ground.

What I did not plan for was Luke Carter.

He is the kind of man who moves a herd three days early without being asked, fixes equipment that isn't his problem, and tells you hard truths about land and life in the same quiet voice. He has been careful about things for a long time, and he has his reasons, and I understand them better than he knows.

The farm is teaching me that the best things grow in the hardest ground. That patience is not waiting -- it is being fully present in the work of the growing season. And that some things know where they are supposed to be before you do.

I came to Twin Arrows to grow flowers.

I did not expect to grow a life.
