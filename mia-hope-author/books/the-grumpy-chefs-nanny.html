The Grumpy Chef's Nanny

A Boss-Nanny Forced Proximity Romance (Cooking with Sexy Grumps)

  • Contemporary Romance
  • Cooking with Sexy Grumps

I’m snowed in with a grumpy single dad as his accidental nanny.

I was just returning a phone. That’s it.

No interview. No résumé. No intention of babysitting a stranger’s kid.

But five minutes in his kitchen and suddenly I’m knee-deep in coloring books, bedtime routines, and a six-year-old who thinks I’m her new best friend.

Her dad?

A broody, infuriatingly hot chef with a spatula in one hand and a trust issue in the other.

Then a snowstorm traps me in their apartment.

One night turns into two. Then pancakes. Then a museum trip.

Then a quiet glass of wine on the couch where he looks at me like I belong here.

But he’s not looking for forever. And I’ve never been good at staying.

Problem is, he’s starting to look like the kind of risk I’d rewrite my whole life for.

Then his ex-shows up, smiling, beautiful, and acting like she never left.

Suddenly I’m the outsider again, and the life I never meant to fall into is on the verge of disappearing.

I’ll be damned if I’m going to let that happen!
