One shot at redemption or my career was literally going up in flames-again-not a good advertisement for any chef.

After a devastating kitchen fire destroyed my reputation three years ago, filming the promo test for a prime-time cooking show is my chance at proving I’m more than the headlines.

Then ingredients vanish, equipment fails, and a falling pan nearly hits me on camera. Someone wants me gone—permanently.

Elise Maddox, the assistant of production, knows the difference between accidents and sabotage.

She discovers I’m being destroyed on purpose.

And we discover each other.

We fight back, together.

Armed with access logs and security footage, we document a conspiracy.

It reaches far deeper than either of us imagined.

The danger escalates and someone knows the fastest way to break me now is through the woman standing beside me.

A gripping romantic suspense where the heat in the kitchen is nothing compared to the danger lurking behind the scenes.
