The Nanny and the Grump

A Slow-Burn Grumpy Boss and Sunshine Nanny Romance (Cooking with Sexy Grumps)

  • Contemporary Romance
  • Cooking with Sexy Grumps

I took the nanny job because I needed a paycheck—not a family.

Gabe Winters is everything I should stay away from. A brooding, sharp-edged celebrity chef with trust issues, scars he doesn’t talk about, and eyes that see right through me. He’s grumpy, guarded and totally closed off from his emotions.

What starts as just a temporary job as a nanny to his six-year-old daughter, Isla, becomes late-night tea in a too-quiet kitchen, soft smiles across pancakes, and a closeness I never expected.

Still, there’s something in the way he reaches for my hand. In the way Isla smiles like I’ve always belonged. And I’m falling for him anyway.

When his ex-wife shows up without warning, I start to wonder if there’s really space for me here—or if I was always meant to be temporary.

Maybe I didn’t come here to fall in love.

But now that I have, I’m not walking away without a fight.
