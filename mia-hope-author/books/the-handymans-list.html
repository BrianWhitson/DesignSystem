The Handyman's List

A Mystic Falls Cozy Mystery (A Mystic Falls Mystery Book 1)

  • Cozy Mystery
  • Mystic Falls Mystery

I came to the North Carolina mountains for a reset. Quiet town. Clean slate. Walk the dog. Read a book. Be no one for a few days.

Instead, my dog found a body.

Abby, my Boykin Spaniel, is search-and-rescue trained, and when she locks onto something, I trust it. What I didn't expect was for that one discovery to pull me into an investigation with Sheriff Deen Lee, and into the quiet, careful work a local handyman had been doing in pencil, in a notebook, under a stack of dish towels. The work he didn't get to finish.

The sheriff is short-handed. The innkeeper has been writing things down for thirty years. The daughter is coming home for the first time in fifteen.

Now I'm in it, with a sheriff who doesn't miss much and a dog who misses nothing, and a town that has been keeping a quiet secret a great deal longer than anyone realized.

I came here for a vacation.

I am beginning to suspect I came here for something else.

The Handyman’s List is a prequel novella to the Mystic Falls Mysteries series. It can be read as a standalone or as an introduction to the quiet town and characters of Mystic Falls, North Carolina.
