I became the nanny to my childhood crush, Ethan Carter, my older brother’s best friend. He’s gorgeous, successful, and completely off-limits.

He’s a single dad. And the grumpiest man alive.

I end up snowed in with him and his daughter, playing house like it's a holiday rom-com. Only this time, I’m the nanny, not the obnoxious little sister. And he’s the one looking at me like he might like what he sees.

It starts with one awkward night, and a tray of burnt cookies. But then it’s story time giggles, accidental touches, and him whispering, “stay”. We both know better.

But I fall hard. For the little girl who radiates sunshine. For the man who swore he’d never fall again.

And loving him might be the bravest—and most dangerous—thing I’ve ever done.

Ethan Carter doesn’t do messy. He doesn’t do me. He won’t let anything, or anyone break his daughter’s heart again…or his, either.
