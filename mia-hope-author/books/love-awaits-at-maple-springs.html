Love Awaits at Maple Springs

A Small Town Clean and Wholesome Romance

  • Clean Small Town Romance
  • Love Awaits

I always thought Maple Springs was the one place that didn’t need saving—until Noah Bennett walked into town with city shoes, big ideas, and no clue what he’s doing!

His aunt’s café was the heartbeat of our community, and now it’s in the hands of a man who thinks espressos and digital menus can fix everything. The town isn’t impressed.

Honestly, neither am I. I have my hands full running my nursery and supplying the town’s restaurants with fresh produce.

But Noah asks for my help. And against my better judgment—and thanks to two meddling Boykin Spaniels—I say yes.

Teaching him our small town’s traditions and the part the café plays should’ve been simple.

Instead… I find myself seeing the man behind the chaos: earnest, driven, unexpectedly kind.

And I find myself falling for him.

But Maple Springs is changing. The café’s future is uncertain. And so is Noah’s place in it.

Will Maple Springs lose the cafe and I lose him?

Love Awaits at Maple Springs is a clean wholesome romance about finding home, building community, and discovering that sometimes the life you didn't plan is exactly the one you need.
