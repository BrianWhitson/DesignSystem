His Inheritance Became our Destiny

I had six weeks to convince Luke Meyers not to sell his uncle's crumbling inn. He had six weeks to escape Willow Creek and return to his real life in the city.

Neither of us planned to fall in love.

But Uncle Henry left more than property. He left secrets carved into foundation stones and hidden in floorboards. Letters revealing an impossible connection between our families that started twenty-five years before we met. He believed it meant something. Spent his final years orchestrating our meeting, hoping we'd choose what he couldn't, love over obligation.

As we restored the Inn room by room, we restored each other. Found home in sawdust and second chances. Built a partnership that became everything.

Then a ruthless developer threatened to steal it all.

Now I'm fighting for the Inn, for Luke, for the town that raised me, and for a love story that was decades in the making.

The real question is: am I brave enough to believe it was always meant to be?
