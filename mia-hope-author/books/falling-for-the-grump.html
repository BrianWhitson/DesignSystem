He’s a single dad in trouble, my brother’s best friend, and a real grumpy jerk!

I only agreed to help out as a favor. Temporary live-in nanny to this guy. Fine. I can handle a brooding, emotionally constipated chef for a few weeks.

But Baker Jackson isn’t just surly; he’s a walking kitchen disaster of rules, routines, and repressed feelings.

His six-year-old daughter, Ellie, is a delight.

Baker? A challenge. A very hot, frustrating, infuriating challenge.

He definitely doesn’t know what to do with someone like me in his space.

Then the kitchen starts heating up with stolen glances… Soft accidental touches… Late night meals, flirty banter, a quick spark, and one unforgettable kiss.

It turns out the real mess isn’t the kid’s craft drawer. It’s our feelings. We’ve become something more than enemies.

I’m falling hard for him. My brother would be furious if he knew…but I’m not going to let him, nor Baker’s ex-wife, nor my ex-business partner wreck what we are building.

I’m going to have this guy, regardless.
