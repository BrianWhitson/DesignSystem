About The Author

Meet Mia Hope, contemporary romance author.

Mia Hope

Mia Hope writes contemporary romance novels that depict believable, relatable people with real-life struggles. Her alpha males are damaged, yet vulnerable. Her heroines are sassy, independent, and strong.

Born and raised in the south, Mia is the eldest of five children. She is a Pickleball addict and gourmet cook. She loves all sports, all animals, most music, reading, camping, and cruising. Mia lives on the Carolina coast with her rescued family members: Boykin Spaniel, Miles, and Spidey the cat.
