About the author

Southern roots, resilient characters, and hopeful endings.

Born and raised in the south, Mia is the eldest of five children. She is a pickleball addict and gourmet cook who loves sports, animals, music, reading, camping, and cruising.

Mia lives on the Carolina coast with her rescued family members: Boykin Spaniel Miles and Spidey the cat. Her mission is to write romance that feels emotionally honest, warm, and deeply connected to readers.
